# Key Findings

- Data-centre metered electricity consumption rose from **1,240 GWh in 2015** to **7,662 GWh in 2025** using the sum of CSO quarterly MEC02 observations — a **6.18× increase** and about **20% CAGR**.
- Data centres rose from about **5.0%** to **23.2%** of metered electricity consumption over the same period.
- Data centres account for roughly **76.6% of the net increase** in metered electricity consumption between 2015 and 2025.
- In rolling-origin validation over 2024–2025, **Holt damped trend** is the best of the four transparent models tested, at roughly **43 GWh RMSE** and **2.0% MAPE**.
- The refitted trend model projects roughly **11.2 TWh/year in 2030** and **14.3 TWh/year in 2035**. These are statistical trend projections, not official forecasts.
- The 2035 projection is about **6.6 TWh/year above 2025**, equivalent to roughly **0.76 GW of additional average continuous load**.
- When normalised to a 2024 index, the model's 2035 growth sits between EirGrid's **Median** and **High** AIRAA scenarios. The underlying units/scopes differ, so this is a growth comparison only.
- Scenario calculations show that the system impact depends strongly on how quickly renewable generation and grid carbon intensity improve alongside demand.
